    Up to 5 photos. Each photo is checked for blur and light before it is sent.

    📸 How to take a good photo
    • Use daylight. Avoid flash, deep shade and strong backlight.
    • Hold the phone steady and tap the screen to focus on the leaf or spot.
    • Fill most of the frame with the plant part. One leaf per photo works best.
    • For disease, show both the top and underside of an affected leaf, plus one healthy-looking leaf if possible.
    • For identification, add a photo of the whole plant and a close-up of a leaf (and flower or fruit if present).
    • For roots, wash off the soil gently before taking the photo.
    • Use a plain background (a sheet of paper or your palm) if leaves are against a busy background.
